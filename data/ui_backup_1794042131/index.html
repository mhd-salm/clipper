Where's your video?

Paste one or more YouTube links, one per line. For each video the AI finds the clips, keeps only the ones rated at or above your minimum score, renders them and uploads them to YouTube by itself. It stops and saves a screenshot if anything goes wrong (daily limit, a Studio change, a problem with a video).

Needs Browser upload to be signed in to Google (do one upload by hand first). Uses the layout, captions and hook settings below. Longer videos give more clips.
