New 2D explainer

Paste a script (from any AI) or write one here. The app adds a free voice, animated 2D scenes, captions and music. Short ones are about 40 seconds; long ones run up to 3 minutes. Scenes change with different transitions, camera moves and backgrounds.

Uses one AI request.
Script format, and a prompt to give another AI

Each scene has the exact words to speak (VOICE) and what to show. Only VOICE is required. Plain narration without any labels also works: it is cut into scenes at the sentence ends.
